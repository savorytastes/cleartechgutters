Why clean gutters?

Keep rainwater moving where it should.

Leaves, twigs, seed pods, and other debris can build up in gutters and interfere with drainage. When water cannot move freely toward the downspouts, it can spill over the gutter edge and repeatedly wet areas around the roofline, siding, walkways, and foundation. Routine gutter cleaning helps restore a clear path for rainwater.

Pittsburgh-area homes often have mature trees and seasonal debris, so gutter buildup can vary substantially from one property to another. ClearTech reviews the home before booking because height, slope, landscaping, gutter layout, and ground access all affect whether our ground-based system is appropriate.
